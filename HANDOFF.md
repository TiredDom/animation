## What exists

- `index.html`: the root composition, 262.73 s (4:22.7, 7,882 frames at 30 fps). One 3D canvas drawn from timeline time
  on `hf-seek` by `assets/film/world.js`. Captions, the HUD and the title and end cards go on top as HTML (not built yet).
- `assets/film/timeline.js`: generated by `python scripts/timeline.py` from the shots and the recorded voice lines:
  every shot's start and end and every line's start, end, speaker and text. Never hard-code film times: shots get
  `lt` (seconds into the shot) and `t` (film time).
- `assets/film/lips.js`: per-frame mouth shapes for every line (`scripts/lips.py`); `person(..., {who})` lip-syncs.
- `assets/film/world.js`: the renderer, Kip's house (`assets/kit/house.js`), the cast (`kip`, `ghostKip`, `mom`,
  `ghostMom`, `cat`, `ghostCat`), props (`controller`, `mug`, `phone`, `marker`, `cupcake`, `laundry`, `coat`,
  `cushions`), Dad's portrait, the mirror, and the camera helpers. `w.reset()` runs before every frame, so each shot
  poses everything it shows from scratch (seek-safe: any frame can be drawn alone).
- `assets/film/stage.js`: staging helpers (`person`, `momAtCounter`, `catAt`, `catOnChair`, `faceTrack`,
  `poseTrack`, `bodyOnCouch`, `headOf`, `onHead`, lens set `L {wide 38, medium 27, close 16}`).
- `assets/film/motion.js`: eases `E` (sine in/out/inOut, `E.cam` cubic), `track(t, keys)`, `ramp(t, a, b, e)`,
  distance-driven walk cycles (`walk`, `walkPhase`, `catWalk`, `catPhase`), `talk`.
- `assets/film/scene1.js` … `scene3.js`: one entry per shot, `{ key, pose(w, lt, t, shot) }` (`key` = the shot's key-pose
  time, used by the lab and boards). `assets/film/shots.js` registers the scenes; unbuilt shots show a placeholder.
- `assets/kit/`: copies of the shared Kip kit (`mascot.js`, `cat.js`, `studio.js`, `house.js`; source of truth is
  `Animations/mascot/` on the laptop). Rig API: `rig.setPose(name|object)`, `rig.setExpression(...)`, `rig.update(t)`
  (breathing, blinks), `rig.solve(pose, {L, R, poleL, poleR})` two-bone arm IK with elbows out, `rig.sitOn(seatY)`,
  `rig.dims`, poses incl. `sit`, `game`, `float`. The house: `w.house.light(...rooms)`, `cutaway(on)`, `morning(on)`,

- `assets/vo/`: the 54 recorded lines (`lines.json` + mp3s). Don't touch them.

## The house rules (the user checks every frame)

- **Natural motion, never bouncy:** slow in and slow out (`E.inOut`, camera `E.cam`), clean start and stop poses, no
  overshoot or springs (the one exception is the comic stretch on the pop-out in shot 2). Anticipation 4–6 frames,
  then the new face, then an 8-frame settle. Offset parts by 2–4 frames: never both arms or both eyes in unison.
- **Nothing hides a face:** no hand, prop, wall edge, caption or foreground object over a face; held things go below
  the chin or beside the head. Never crop a head at the top of the frame unless it is a deliberate extreme close-up.
- **Clarity:** one action per shot; medium shots and close-ups on what people do; the subject big and in frame;
  props and text that matter face the camera long enough to read. Camera variety is wanted (top views, low angles,
  over-the-shoulder, POV), but every frame must read at a glance.
- **No black or blank frames:** no cuts to black, no dips, and no camera inside geometry (the flat grey or red frames
  in scene 3 are exactly this). The first frame of every shot shows the picture.
- **Contact:** real bodies stand on the floor (no floating, no sinking); feet stay planted while standing; walks are
  driven by distance (`walkPhase`) so feet never slide or moonwalk; stairs are climbed tread by tread. Ghosts hover at
  least 10 cm and pass through walls. Never fold a body to reach: stage the distance, stay tall, step in. Round nub
  hands drawn over what they hold (IK contact with `rig.solve`), elbows pointing out from the body.
- **Alive:** everyone breathes and blinks (`rig.update(t)`) except the two frozen bodies (Kip's, and Mom's after she
  turns ghost), which stay frozen.
- **Facing logic:** characters face what they look at; eyelines match across cuts; screen direction holds across a cut.
- **Ghosts:** see-through, tinted, never glowing; no part of a ghost shows through another (the ghost pass handles it:
  put ghosts on the ghost rigs, never fake them with opacity on a normal rig).
- **Design is locked:** don't change how Kip, Mom or the cat look. New poses, expressions, props and rig fixes are fine.
